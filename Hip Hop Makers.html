The Music Maker WordPress theme just received a new update with 20 brand-new demo styles and new features that make building a professional music website even easier.

What Is the Music Maker Theme?

The Music Maker Theme is a premium WordPress theme designed for music producers, artists, and beat makers who want to showcase their work, sell beats, and grow their online presence. It’s easy to customize, optimized for mobile, and built to help you look professional fast — without needing to know any code.

If you haven’t checked it out in a while, now’s the perfect time. The latest updates bring new designs, better performance, and new tools to personalize your music brand. Learn More

20 New Demo Styles

The new version introduces 20 all-new demo styles that can be easily imported when setting up your website. Each demo gives you a unique look and layout — from dark and minimalist designs to bright, bold styles inspired by top producer websites.

These demos make it simple to launch your site in minutes. Just pick a demo, import it, and customize it to match your brand’s vibe. Click the preview images to view live demos.

New & Improved Features

Recent updates have added several powerful new features and improvements:

2-Color Site Title: Add a highlight color to part of your site title for a bold, professional look.

  • 20 New Demo Styles: Easily import a design that fits your music brand.
  • Photo Gallery Widget Fixed: Improved background image settings for cleaner visuals.
  • Improved Beat Store Image Quality: Beats now look sharper in the beat player and overlay window.
  • Added 27 New Google Fonts: Including popular modern fonts like Inter Tight, Lexend, League Spartan, and Roboto Flex.
  • Bug Fixes: Polished visuals and performance across frontpage widgets and beat player settings.
  • Additional Google Fonts: Added Bebas Neue, Space Grotesk, and Abril Face.
  • Design Updates: New color options for the footer, improved promo bar text size, and flexible banner height options.
