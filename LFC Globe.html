Liverpool 0-0 Fulham Player Ratings: Jacquet Excels in Anfield Stalemate

Jacob
12th September 2026
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 2:15
Instaread

Liverpool played well defensively, earning a clean sheet, but the attacking players could not produce much going forward in a disappointing 0-0 draw with Fulham. In fact, Liverpool only managed three shots on goal in the 90 minutes on their way to a fourth straight draw at Anfield

  • Alisson (6/10): As usual, solid between the pipes
  • Van Dijk (7/10): The defense wasn’t the issue today. Van Dijk did his part on the back line.
  • Jacquet (8/10): Saved a goal in the first half, strong in individual battles 
  • Tsimikas (4/10):  Rare start and did not take advantage of the opportunity
  • Wirtz (6/10): This is a theme for all the players going forward. Put himself in good positions, but could not do anything with it
  • Szoboszlai (5/10): Quiet game, which unfortunately was the theme for the day for Liverpool  
  • Isak (7/10):  A couple of good chances, but could not find the finishing touch 
  • Barcola (7/10): Good game. Nearly set up Isak for a goal.
  • Araújo (6/10): Good in the air, winning the ball, not much else.
  • Gravenberch (5/10): Gave away a few balls. I expected better with fresh legs. 
  • Munoz (6/10): Best chance of the game with a header that was saved off the bar.

Substitutes

  • Gakpo (5/10):  A bad pass ended a promising attack for the Reds
  • Mac Allister (7/10): Attacking mindset as soon as he stepped on the pitch
  • Kerkez (6/10): Drew a dangerous free kick late
  • Ngumoha (5/10): Needed to be a bigger spark off the bench
  • Frimpong (5/10): Name barely called after coming on

Man of the Match

Jacquet

The Manager 

  • Andoni Iraola (7/10): I think the boss made all the right moves in terms of starting XI and subs. The players just did not execute. 

LFC Globe Verdict

This is another disappointing result for Liverpool. Yes, the Reds are still unbeaten in all competitions, but for this club, 3 points are expected with a lot more regularity.
