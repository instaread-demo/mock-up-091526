Blog anniversary | ‘Reel Librarians’ blog turns 15!

Celebrating 15 years of love for reel librarians!

Fifteen years ago on September 19, 2011, I published my very first post, “Where do I begin? A love story” on Reel Librarians. That post also explains the background about my 20+ years interest in analyzing portrayals of librarians in film. And every year on my blog anniversary, I compare the blog stats through the years, as well as review this past year’s most popular posts. Let’s dig in and reflect, shall we?

Quick stats

Here is an update on how this blog has grown over the years. I added the total word count category in recent years, because I thought it was fun to document how my posts have gotten wordier over the years! 😀

Most popular posts the past 15 years

For this list, I reviewed the previous anniversary posts for perennially popular posts. This method revealed a lot of surprises!

  • Marian or Marion?: Originally published May 2012, this post has made the blog anniversary’s Top 10 list a total of 10 different times, from 2013-2020 and then again from 2022-2023. This post really surprised me with its longevity!
  • One of the Hidden Figures posts: The “First impressions: ‘Hidden Figures’ and its library scene” post published in Feb. 2017 made the Top 10 list a total of 5 times, and the follow-up “A closer look at the library scene in ‘Hidden Figures’ (2016)” post published in March 2021 made the Top 10 list a total of 6 times. The two posts also overlapped in the same year, both making the Top 10 list in 2021, so one or both of the Hidden Figures posts has earned a Top 10 spot in 10 different years. Amazing!
  • Books and book-burning in ‘Fahrenheit 451’ : This post has made the Top 10 list since it was originally published May 2017, a total of 9 times — and it’s never been ranked lower than #4 on the list since its publication.
  • These posts tied with 8 appearances each on the Top 10 list:
    • Angels in the library in ‘Wings of Desire’ – originally published Jan. 2018
    • ‘You, Me and Dupree’ — and the Naughty Librarian – originally published Aug. 2015
    • Naughty Librarians (ladies, take it away) – originally published Mar. 2012
  • These posts tied with 7 appearances each on the Top 10 list:
    • First impressions: ‘Monsters University’ – originally published July 2013
    • Librarian t-shirt collection – originally published Sept. 2014
  • These posts tied with 6 appearances each on the Top 10 list:
    • ‘It’s a wonderful’… stereotype? – originally published Sept. 2011, the very first post on the blog didn’t actually start making the Top 10 list until 2021!
    • The dragon lady librarian in ‘The Golden Child’ (1986) – originally published June 2019
    • The Jedi librarian – originally published Mar. 2013
    • ‘The Killing Kind’ vs. ‘The Attic’ – originally published Oct. 2013

Thank you all for reading, whether it’s your first or fifteenth year! ❤️

What is Reel Librarians?

There are hundreds of examples of librarians in movies, from bit parts to protagonists. On this site, we explore the gamut of reel librarians — librarian portrayals in movies — particularly the purpose of librarian roles, character types, and stereotypical traits.

Visit the About page to read more about the real-life librarian behind this site and blog.
