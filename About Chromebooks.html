    Two Forrester studies commissioned by Google carry most of the published Chromebook total cost of ownership statistics, and they measure different fleets over different horizons. A Jamf-commissioned analysis reaches the opposite conclusion on resale value. This article sets all three against Microsoft’s Windows 10 ESU list pricing and Omdia’s 2026 component-cost figures.

    What Do The Forrester Chromebook Total Cost Of Ownership Statistics Show?

    Forrester interviewed six decision-makers and built one composite organisation: a multinational with $5 billion in annual revenue and 40,000 employees. Ten thousand of them move to ChromeOS on 3,000 shared devices.

    That composite returns $10.09 million in three-year risk-adjusted benefits against $3.27 million in costs, giving a $6.81 million net present value and a payback under six months. Forrester discounts at 10%.

    Benefit lineThree-year PVUnderlying metric
    Increased end-user productivity$6,468,30290,000 hours a year
    Lowered device and licence costs$1,298,272$500 per device
    Strengthened security$1,227,79490% incremental risk cut
    Reduced IT support needs$1,093,6352,880 to 1,080 tickets
    Total benefits$10,088,003vs $3,273,277 costs

    Source: Forrester Consulting, The Total Economic Impact of Google ChromeOS, commissioned by Google, September 2025.

    Productivity carries the largest line and rests on the softest input. Forrester models 18 hours saved per employee per year at a fully burdened $34 an hour, then recaptures only 50% of it, so nothing here is removed headcount.

    The support line is more traceable. Average ticket resolution falls from two hours to 15 minutes, monthly ticket rates drop from 8% of devices to 2%, and the model removes two endpoint manager roles at $115,000 each. Hardware and licence patterns behind the $500 figure also show up in Chromebook adoption across remote and hybrid workforces.

    Chromebook Total Cost Of Ownership Statistics For K-12 Districts

    The education study is separate, older and longer. Forrester interviewed 18 representatives at nine school organisations and modelled a district of 30,000 students, 1,200 teachers and 60 IT staff over five years.

    Benefits reach $35.12 million against $10.66 million in costs, for a $24.45 million NPV and 229% ROI. One line does most of the work.

    Source: Forrester Consulting, The Total Economic Impact of Chromebooks in Education, commissioned by Google, January 2024.

    Avoided hardware runs on two levers, not one. Chromebooks are priced below the legacy devices they replace, and Forrester stretches the replacement interval at the same time. Device management time falls 76%, cutting the staff needed for student and teacher endpoints from 25 to six.

One North American district reported 50% fewer support tickets after the move, and an APAC school system cut annual device management from 420 hours to under five.     Chromebook Total Cost Of Ownership Statistics Against Windows 10 ESU

    Windows 10 reached end of support on 14 October 2025. Microsoft sells Extended Security Updates through volume licensing at $61 per device for Year One and states the price doubles every consecutive year, for a maximum of three years.

    Buyers cannot purchase partial periods, and the licences are cumulative. An organisation enrolling in Year Two pays for Year One as well.

    Windows 10 ESU, commercialPer deviceBasis
    Year One$61Microsoft list price
    Year Two$122Published doubling rule
    Year Three$244Published doubling rule

    Source: Microsoft, Extended Security Updates program for Windows 10, page updated 17 November 2025.

    Google lists ChromeOS Enterprise Upgrade at $4.17 per device per month and the Kiosk and Signage Upgrade at $2.08, both as standalone annual purchases. Forrester’s enterprise model uses $50 per device per year for the same licence.

    ESU buys critical and important security patches only. No features, no technical support. The comparison therefore holds for organisations keeping a working Windows 10 machine rather than replacing it, and running-cost gaps beyond licensing appear in this ChromeOS versus Windows power consumption breakdown.

    How 2026 Memory Prices Move Chromebook Total Cost Of Ownership

    Omdia put the added cost of mainstream memory and storage configurations at US$90 to US$165 per PC since the first quarter of 2025. It expects a minimum 60% further rise in 1Q26.

    That is a fixed dollar amount per device, not a percentage, and Omdia says the impact across PC product categories is broadly consistent. Worldwide shipments of desktops, notebooks and workstations are forecast to fall 12% to 245 million units in 2026.

    Platform2026 forecast shipment change
    Chrome devices−28%
    Windows PCs−12%
    Macs−5%

    Source: Omdia press release, Global PC shipments to decline 12% in 2026, 10 March 2026.

    Omdia’s Kieren Jessop attributes the Chrome device figure to tighter component allocation, lower margins and the discontinuation of some memory and storage products on an education-heavy platform. PCs priced below US$500 are forecast to drop 28% to around 62.1 million units, the same band most school Chromebook fleets buy from.

    Where Chromebook Total Cost Of Ownership Statistics Disagree

    A Diamond Assets and Jamf analysis published 1 November 2024 reverses the usual ranking. It puts three-year total cost of ownership at $194 for an iPad against $244 for a Chromebook.

    The mechanism is residual value rather than purchase price.

    Per device, USDiPadChromebook
    Three-year total cost of ownership$194$244
    Trade-in value at two years$145$10
    Trade-in value at four years$80$0

    Source: Diamond Assets and Jamf, TCO: iPad vs Chromebook for schools, November 2024.

    Jamf also cites a Township High School District 211 report: Chromebook damage at four times the iPad rate, touchscreen repair costs twice as high, a required surplus fleet of 20% against 5%, and roughly $6 more per student per year in device management. Serviceability data across models sits in these Chromebook repairability scores.

    Both sides are vendor-funded, and neither publishes device-level residuals for the competing platform. On the published evidence, Chromebook cost advantages sit in acquisition price, licensing and IT labour, not in asset recovery. Broader platform figures are collected in these Chromebook usage statistics.

    FAQs

    How much does a Chromebook cost to own over three years?

    Diamond Assets and Jamf put three-year Chromebook total cost of ownership at $244 per device, against $194 for an iPad, in November 2024. Forrester’s separate enterprise model credits $500 in combined hardware and licence savings per device.

    Is a Chromebook cheaper than a Windows laptop?

    Forrester’s September 2025 composite saved $300 per device on hardware and $200 a year on avoided endpoint software. Microsoft charges $61 per device for Year One of Windows 10 Extended Security Updates, doubling each following year.

    How much is ChromeOS Enterprise Upgrade?

    Google lists the standalone ChromeOS Enterprise Upgrade at $4.17 per device per month, sold annually. The Kiosk and Signage Upgrade lists at $2.08. Bundled Chromebook Enterprise devices carry a perpetual upgrade instead.

    What do Reddit threads say about Chromebook total cost of ownership?

    Reddit posts are self-selected anecdotes, not measured data, and no published dataset tracks them. The modelled figures come from Forrester: 208% three-year ROI and $6.8 million NPV for a 40,000-employee composite organisation, September 2025.

    How long does a school Chromebook last?

    Forrester’s district model replaces student Chromebooks every five years and teacher Chromebook Plus units every six, against three and four years for the legacy devices they replace. Districts budgeted $250 to $400 per Chromebook.
