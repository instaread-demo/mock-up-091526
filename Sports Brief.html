Frank Onyeka Signs Permanent Coventry City Deal After Helping Club Win Championship Title and Promotion

Nigeria international Frank Onyeka has completed a permanent transfer to Coventry City from Brentford for an undisclosed fee, having played a key role in the club's Championship title-winning campaign under Frank Lampard.

Coventry City have secured the permanent signing of Super Eagles midfielder Frank Onyeka from Brentford, with the club officially confirming the deal on June 29, 2026. The transfer, which takes effect from July 1, ends any uncertainty over the 28-year-old’s future following a loan spell at the Coventry Building Society Arena that began in January.

Onyeka arrived at Coventry midway through last season and made an immediate impact, featuring in 14 Championship matches during the second half of the campaign. His performances contributed directly to the club’s title-winning finish under manager Frank Lampard, with Coventry securing automatic promotion back to the Premier League in the process.

A highlight of his loan stint came in April, when Onyeka scored a crucial goal against Derby County in a fixture that proved significant in consolidating Coventry’s promotion push. That performance, among others, was enough to persuade the club to exercise a permanent deal once the summer transfer window opened.

Prior to his move to Coventry, Onyeka had accumulated 75 appearances for Brentford since joining the west London club, giving him considerable experience at Premier League level. That top-flight background is expected to be an asset for Coventry as they navigate their return to England’s top division.

Speaking after the deal was completed, Onyeka reflected on what he set out to achieve when he first arrived on loan. “I’m really excited for the new season with Coventry. When I first joined last season, I came here with two goals: to win the Championship and help Coventry get promoted to the Premier League. I’m really happy to now be a Coventry player back in the Premier League,” he said.

The Nigerian international also outlined his objectives for the season ahead. “I will give everything to the team, fight for every ball, fight for my teammates and bring my experience. My ambition now is to help the club stay in the Premier League and finish as high as we can,” Onyeka added.

Coventry’s return to the Premier League ends a three-year absence from the top flight, and strengthening their midfield with an experienced operator like Onyeka signals the club’s intent to establish themselves in the division. The Championship title, secured under Lampard, marked a significant turnaround for a club that has spent much of the past decade in the second tier.

For Onyeka, the permanent move represents a new chapter in a career that has seen him become a regular fixture in the Super Eagles setup. His combination of defensive solidity and technical quality in central midfield aligns with the demands of Premier League football, and Coventry will be looking to him as one of their key figures when the new season gets underway.
